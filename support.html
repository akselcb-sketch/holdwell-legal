Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

Holdwell is not medical advice. Follow your doctor’s instructions.

Contact us

Email akselcb@gmail.com. We read every message.

It helps if you tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and the Holdwell version (Settings in the app, then About). Please do not send more health information than you need to explain the problem.

For a medical question, ask your doctor or pharmacist. We cannot answer medical questions.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Holdwell and tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with. This is how you get Holdwell back after you reinstall the app or move to a new iPhone. If nothing happens, check that you are signed in to the right Apple Account in iPhone Settings, then email us.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose Holdwell and tap Cancel Subscription. See Apple’s step-by-step guide. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. If you are in the 7-day free trial, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends to avoid being charged. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds, so we cannot refund a purchase ourselves. You can request a refund from Apple.

Where is my data, and is it private?

Your shots, protein, workouts, weights and photos are stored in the app on your iPhone. There is no Holdwell account and we do not keep a copy. Apple Health data is never sold, shared or used for advertising. Only a meal photo you choose to analyze, a barcode number you scan, and your purchase information leave your phone. There are no ads and no tracking. Read the full Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

Because your data is on your phone, deleting it is up to you. Delete single entries in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stores on your phone. If you used iCloud Backup, an older backup can still hold a copy until you delete that backup in iPhone Settings, your name, iCloud, Manage Account Storage, Backups. Weights, protein and workouts that Holdwell wrote to Apple Health stay in the Health app until you delete them there.

How do I move Holdwell to a new iPhone?

Your data moves with your phone backup or with Apple’s Quick Start transfer. Then open Holdwell and tap Restore Purchases. Holdwell has no account, so there is nothing to sign in to.

Why doesn’t Holdwell suggest doses?

Your dose is a medical decision between you and your doctor. Holdwell logs what you took and when. It never recommends, suggests or adjusts a dose, and it has no dose charts or “next dose” advice. When you log a shot, the only thing Holdwell fills in is the number you typed last time, so you do not have to retype it. Change it any time. If you have questions about your dose, ask your doctor or pharmacist.

How is my protein target worked out?

Doctors commonly suggest 1.2 to 1.6 grams of protein per kilogram of body weight a day while losing weight, together with strength training 2 to 3 times a week. Holdwell multiplies a reference weight by an amount in that range. The reference weight is your goal weight when it is below your current weight, otherwise your current weight. The amount goes up a little if you are 50 or older or already train regularly. The result is rounded to 5 grams. You can set your own target in Settings, and the explanation is on the Protein tab. If your doctor or dietitian gave you a different target, use theirs.

What is the Muscle Score?

It is a habit score from 0 to 100 for your last 7 days: protein (45 points), strength sessions (35 points) and the pace of your weight change (20 points). Tap the score in the app to see how it was made. It is not a medical measurement and it does not measure your muscle.

Do I have to connect Apple Health?

No. Holdwell works without it, and you choose each data type when iOS asks. You can change access any time in the Health app. See the Apple Health section of the Privacy Policy.

The meal photo estimate does not work

The photo estimate needs an internet connection and an active subscription, and it has a daily limit. If it is unavailable you can always log protein with quick add, a barcode or by typing it in. Estimates are approximate: check the numbers before you log them. See how photos are handled in the Privacy Policy.

The barcode scanner does not work

Allow camera access for Holdwell in iPhone Settings. The live scanner needs an iPhone that supports it, so on other devices you can type the barcode number instead. If a product is not found, it is not in the Open Food Facts database yet: type the protein from the label.

My reminders do not arrive

Allow notifications for Holdwell in iPhone Settings and check that Focus modes are not silencing them. Reminders are created on your phone, so they also work offline. Do not rely on a reminder to take your medication.

Which medications can I track?

Ozempic, Wegovy, Wegovy pill, Mounjaro and Zepbound, or any other medication under “Other”. Weekly shots and daily pills are supported. Holdwell is not affiliated with the makers of these medications.

I feel unwell during a workout

Stop. If you feel dizzy, faint, short of breath, or have chest pain, severe nausea or vomiting, or pain, stop exercising and get medical help. In an emergency call your local emergency number (911 in the United States). Ask your doctor before you start or continue an exercise program.
